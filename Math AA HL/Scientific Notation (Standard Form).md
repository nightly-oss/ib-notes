Any number in the form of $a \cdot 10^k$ where $1 \le a < 10$ and k is an integer is written in Standard Form. For example. $5.2 \cdot 10^{3}$ is $520$

Related: [[Rounding]]

---

> [!TIP] Practice problem
> Q: Sheets of paper are $3.2 \cdot 10^{-4}m$ thick. How many sheets are required to make a pile of paper higher than 10cm?
> A (final): $313$
